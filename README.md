# AeroTwin – Aircraft Material & Maintenance Atlas

**Live demo:** https://aybuaerotwin.github.io/

AeroTwin is an interactive, browser-based 3D aircraft atlas for aircraft maintenance education. Students explore a passenger aircraft part by part, see which material each part is made of and why, and practise maintenance and inspection procedures step by step on the 3D model.

## Features
- 3D aircraft model with clickable structural parts and a material datasheet for each
- Maintenance module (ATA 57 – Wings): task cards, access panels, choice of inspection method (visual, detailed visual, eddy current, ultrasonic, magnetic particle), calibration, 3D scan, findings and sign-off
- Guided tour, quiz mode and Turkish / English interface
- Runs in any modern browser; no installation needed

## Important: training demo
Maintenance values in this demo (torques, limits, pressures, consumable codes) are **illustrative only** and have been deliberately altered. They must not be used on aircraft. Always use the current, approved Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM).

## Project
Developed at the Aircraft Technology Programme, Vocational School of Technical Sciences, Ankara Yıldırım Beyazıt University (Türkiye). The atlas is the starting point of a planned Erasmus+ KA220-VET cooperation partnership on digital twins for aircraft maintenance training. Interested VET schools, training organisations and companies are welcome to get in touch.
